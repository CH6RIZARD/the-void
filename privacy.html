PRIVACY POLICY

What we collect, and what we never do

EFFECTIVE 16 SEPTEMBER 2026

The short version. The Void has no ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking and no data brokers. We store what you post and the account you need to post it. DMs are stored on our server and are not end-to-end encrypted. The optional body-signals feature runs entirely on your phone: camera frames and heart-rate readings are never stored or uploaded.

Who we are

The Void ("we", "us") is a news and social app for iOS and Android and a web app at void-app-d35c.pages.dev. This policy covers all of them. Questions go to daniel4chigs@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Anonymous account. The first time you post, reply, react or message, the app creates an anonymous account so the action has an owner. It holds a random user ID and nothing about who you are.
  • Email, Google or Apple sign-in (optional). If you use one, our authentication provider stores your email address and the link to that sign-in method. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email. For Apple accounts the app keeps an Apple token on your device so your Apple link can be revoked when you delete your account.
  • Profile. Your handle, display name and avatar, and when you accepted the terms. Your profile is public.

What you create

  • Posts, replies, reactions and follows. Public by design.
  • Media you upload. Photos, videos and voice notes you attach are stored in our file storage. Anyone with a file's link can open it.
  • Direct messages. Message text and membership of each conversation are stored on our server so they reach the other person. Only conversation members can read them through the app, but they are not end-to-end encrypted: they are encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider, and someone with administrative access to the database could technically read them. Do not send anything you need to keep truly secret.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report a post, reply or account, we store the target, the reason and any note you add. When you block someone, we store that block so it can be enforced.
  • Notifications generated inside the app (for example, a reply to your post).

Stored only on your device

Preferences (theme, language, globe mode, feed layout), muted keywords, hidden sources, which posts you have already seen, reading-interest weights used to order your feed, reports waiting to be sent after sign-in, your sign-in session, and your body-signals consent choices. These stay in the app's storage (or your browser's local storage on the web) and are removed when you delete the app, clear site data, or use Settings → Clear All Data.

What we do not collect

No precise or coarse location, no contacts, no advertising ID, no browsing history outside the app, no payment data. We do not read what you type anywhere except the text you choose to post or send.

Third-party content and embeds

Most of the feed is public content fetched from other services. When the app loads it, your device (or our proxy, below) contacts those services and they receive the usual request data such as your IP address and device/browser type. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Bluesky (public API and media CDN), Mastodon and other fediverse servers, Lemmy and PieFed instances.
  • Reddit, fetched through our proxy when it is enabled.
  • GDELT Project (news events), PeerTube instances via SepiaSearch, Radio Browser (radio stations), Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons.
  • YouTube. Live streams and videos play in YouTube's embedded player. YouTube (Google) may set cookies, collect device data and log what you watch under Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms. We do not receive that data.
  • Live TV and city cameras are streamed directly from the broadcasters' and camera operators' own servers.
  • Translation. If you tap Translate on a post, that post's text is sent to Google Translate to be translated.
  • Web app only: fonts load from Google Fonts, and the hls.js and three.js libraries load from the jsDelivr and cdnjs CDNs.

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their practices.

Our proxy

Some feeds are fetched through a small server we run, so your device does not contact those sites directly. It keeps fetched public posts in memory for about a minute. It does not store who asked for them. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (IP address, time, URL requested) for a short period for security and operations.

Body signals (optional, on-device)

The Void can tune your feed to how you react to posts. This is off unless you choose it on the consent screen, and each part is switched on separately:

  • How you hold and scroll: scroll speed, pauses, taps and how still the phone is. No permission needed. We never read what you type.
  • Front camera: while the feed is open, estimates blinks, whether you are looking at the screen, smiles, and a rough pulse from skin colour.
  • Fingertip calm reading: only when you start it, the rear camera and flash measure heart rate and heart-rate variability for 30 seconds.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate strap or watch: if you pair one, the app reads the heart rate it broadcasts while the app is open.

Processed in memory, never stored, never uploaded. Camera frames, motion data and heart-rate readings are processed on your phone and discarded. They are never saved, never sent to us or anyone else, and never used for advertising or sold. Only a few rounded, noise-added scores (attention, arousal, mood) are passed to the feed ranking on the same device. Face detection uses an on-device model; no image leaves the phone.

You stay in control. Turn it off at any time in Settings → Body signals, which acts as a kill switch and stops all capture immediately. You can also withdraw camera or Bluetooth access in your phone's settings.

A wellness estimate, not a medical device. These readings are rough estimates for personalising a feed. They are not intended to diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Do not use them for health decisions.

Where the law treats this as biometric or health data (for example GDPR Article 9, Illinois BIPA, Washington's My Health My Data Act), your explicit opt-in on the consent screen is the basis for processing, and because nothing is retained there is nothing for us to hold, share or delete.

Device permissions and why

  • Camera: for body signals if you turn them on, and to take a photo for a post if you choose to.
  • Microphone: only when you tap record in the composer to attach a voice note. Nothing is recorded at any other time.
  • Photos / media library: only to pick the photos or videos you attach. The app does not browse your library.
  • Bluetooth: only to connect a heart-rate strap or watch you choose to pair.

Every permission is asked for at the moment it is needed, and you can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

How we use and share data

  • We use your data only to run The Void: show your posts, deliver messages, enforce blocks, review reports and keep the service secure.
  • No ads. No sale of personal data. No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. No analytics or advertising SDKs are built into the app.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Cloudflare (web hosting) and the host of our proxy process data on our behalf. Google and Apple handle sign-in if you use them.
  • We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect people from serious harm, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

Where data is stored

Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the United States (AWS region us-east-2, Ohio). If you use The Void from outside the US, your data is transferred there. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Where required, transfers rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, posts, media, messages, follows and blocks: until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports: until they are reviewed and closed, and while your account exists. Evidence of illegal content may be kept longer where the law requires.
  • Content removed by moderation is deleted from the database.
  • Deleted data can remain in our provider's encrypted backups until those backups expire, and is not restored from them.
  • Body-signal data: not retained at all.
  • Device-local data: until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. On the web: void-app-d35c.pages.dev/account-deletion. Anonymous accounts can be deleted too.

Deletion removes your login, profile, posts and replies (and replies others made to them), uploaded media, reactions, follows, notifications, messages and conversations you were in, reports and blocks. It happens right away and cannot be undone. Messages you sent are removed for the other person as well.

You can also email us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to processing. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you have these rights by law, and you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The Void is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Because the feed carries unfiltered news (which can include graphic images of war and violence) and content posted by users, the app is rated 17+ on the App Store and 18+ / Mature on Google Play. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app before a material change takes effect.
