ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your account and your data

In the app

  1. Open THE VOID and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the settings gear.
  3. In Identity, tap DELETE on the "Delete account" row.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion happens right away, and it also works for anonymous accounts. Once it finishes, you are signed out and the app's data on your device is erased.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, handle and display name
  • Your posts and replies, their media, and replies others wrote under them
  • Your ranks and slashes (scores are corrected), follows and followers
  • Your messages, conversations, and device encryption keys
  • Notifications, reports and blocks you made, and synced taste data
  • Your login (email, Google or Apple link, or anonymous identity)

Nothing is kept for later recovery. Server backups age out on the hosting provider's normal backup schedule.

No longer have the app?

Sign in with the method you used in the app, then delete your account here. Anonymous accounts have no credentials, so they can only be deleted in the app on the device that created them. If that device is gone, the anonymous account can't be reached by anyone, including you.
