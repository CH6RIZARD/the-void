# Store listing — The Void 1.0.0

Copy for Google Play (and reusable for the App Store). Character counts were checked with a script; limits are Play's.

## App name (≤30)

```
The Void: Unfiltered News
```
25 characters. Plain `The Void` (8) also fits. The suffix helps search, because "The Void" alone collides with many games and meditation apps. Keep the in-app `expo.name` as `The Void`; the store title can be longer than the launcher label.

## Category

**Google Play: News & Magazines. App Store: primary News, secondary Social Networking.**

Why: the core loop (ranked feed, nation pages, the live news wall, GDELT events) is news. The social features (posts, replies, DMs) are there to argue about that news. Picking News & Magazines:
- matches what reviewers will see on first launch, so there is less risk of a "misleading category" rejection;
- competes in a much smaller category than Social;
- does not change the obligations. Play's News declaration and the UGC rules apply because of what the app does, whatever the category.

Tags to add in Play Console (Store presence > Store settings > Tags): News, World news, Social, Politics.
